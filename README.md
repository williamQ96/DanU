# 🎓 Dan University — Cybersecurity Meme & Learning Hub

Welcome to **Dan University**, a meme site turned educational resource base, built as a tribute to our legendary cybersecurity instructor. What started as an inside joke has grown into an open-source platform for cybersecurity learning tools, community contributions, and practical self-study.

[🌐 Live Website](https://pages.uoregon.edu/williamq/)

---

## 🧠 About the Project

Dan University is a parody-styled website inspired by our beloved instructor Dan, known for his famous phrase:  
> _"You just graduated from Dan University!"_

Originally designed for fun, the project quickly grew into something more meaningful:  
✅ A showcase of my web development skills  
✅ A curated portal of cybersecurity learning resources  
✅ A home for open-source tools and links to major frameworks  
✅ A community platform for students, hackers, and future cyber defenders

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript
