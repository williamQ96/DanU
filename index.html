About Dan U

Dan U isn’t your typical university—it’s a place where knowledge meets humor, and every right answer gets you one step closer to 'graduation.' Built around cybersecurity expertise and hands-on learning, Dan U is designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare students for the ever-evolving digital landscape.

Cybersecurity isn’t learned through theory alone. At Dan U, students engage in real-world simulations, ethical hacking exercises, and security labs to gain practical expertise.
